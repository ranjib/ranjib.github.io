The durable subscription path for this site starts with RSS. It works without JavaScript, does not require a social platform account, and keeps the canonical article archive here.

## RSS

Use the site feed:

[Subscribe with RSS](/index.xml)

RSS readers can follow new essays as they are published. The feed includes stable canonical URLs, publication dates, and short descriptions for each item.

## Monthly Email Digest

The working digest name is **Field Notes on Reliable Systems**. It should curate the latest essay, one older related piece, one maker or field note, and one short learning note.

The email digest is not collecting addresses yet. A provider will be selected only after the consent, unsubscribe, export, archive, and privacy model is approved.

## Privacy Posture

The subscription path should remain portable and minimal: no behavioral advertising, no selling or sharing subscriber data, no hidden tracking pixels by default, and no provider lock-in without an export path.
