This site is the public home for my writing and project notes. The through-line is systems in practice: making intent explicit, instrumenting reality, and using feedback loops across software, infrastructure, AI work, open-source hardware, and field projects.

I write here in a personal capacity. The views are my own, and the site avoids private family logistics, confidential employer details, and material that belongs in private notes.

## A First Reading Path

Start with these pieces if you want the shape of the site quickly.

1. [Context Engineering Evals Remind Me of Infrastructure Testing](/posts/context-engineering-evaluation-and-infrastructure-testing-lessons/) connects agent evaluation, infrastructure testing, and reliability habits.
2. [The Through-Line From Chef to Agentic AI](/posts/through-line-from-chef-to-agentic-ai/) traces the recurring pattern from infrastructure automation to context engineering.
3. [reef-pi as a Small Open Source Platform](/posts/reef-pi-as-a-small-open-source-platform/) shows how a hobby controller became a maintainable public project.
4. [Planning Overlanding Trips Like Reliability Reviews](/posts/planning-overlanding-trips-like-reliability-reviews/) applies reliability thinking to real-world route planning and field constraints.

## Main Lanes

**Engineering & AI** covers production engineering, infrastructure automation, incident learning, agent evaluation, context engineering, and observability.

**Open Source & Making** covers reef-pi, Raspberry Pi automation, physical computing, documentation, and building public projects that other people can operate.

**Field Notes** covers outdoor planning, gardening, overlanding, aviation, astronomy, and other places where systems thinking meets changing physical conditions.

**Learning & Life** covers knowledge systems, GenAI workflows, teaching, reflection, and the operating model behind durable personal learning.
