## About Section

I am Ranjib Dey, a software engineer focused on production engineering, reliability, infrastructure automation, reliable AI systems, and open source systems.

My public work started with Chef, infrastructure as code, and testing discipline for operations. It continued through Linux containers, service discovery, incident management, resiliency engineering, and ML-assisted capacity management. Today I work on production engineering at Uber, and I write publicly in a personal capacity about reliable systems and the habits that make them easier to operate.

The through-line is Systems in Practice: making intent explicit, testing behavior at the right layer, building feedback loops, and learning from real systems.

Outside work, I build reef-pi, an open source Raspberry Pi based reef tank controller, and I write about physical systems, field planning, gardening, and learning when there is a useful systems lesson.

Personal writing and public work: https://ranjib.github.io/

Views are my own and do not represent my employer.

## Follow Vs. Connect

Recommendation: use **Follow** as the primary public action and keep Connect for people with a concrete shared context.

Reasoning: the website and distribution strategy are designed for public readership and conversation. Follow supports that without turning every reader into a connection request.
