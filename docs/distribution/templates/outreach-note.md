# Selective Outreach Note Template

Use only when the article strongly matches the recipient or community.

```markdown
Hi <name>,

I published a short piece on <specific topic> that may be relevant to <specific public discussion, project, or shared context>.

The useful bit is <one-sentence contribution or question>.

Canonical link:
<campaign URL with source/medium appropriate to the outreach surface>

I would value critique if this misses something from your experience.
```

Rules:

- Send manually.
- No automated DMs, comments, connection requests, or engagement.
- Do not imply an obligation to respond.
- Do not include private notes, private relationship context, or confidential employer context.
- Prefer a request for critique over a request for amplification.
