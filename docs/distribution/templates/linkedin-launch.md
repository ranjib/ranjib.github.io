# LinkedIn Launch Template

Use this for the first post after a canonical essay goes live.

```markdown
<First-hand hook grounded in a concrete project, time, or lesson.>

<Standalone insight: explain the useful idea without requiring the click.>

<Bridge to the canonical article and why it is worth reading.>

Read it here:
<campaign URL with utm_content=launch>

Question:
<One substantive question that invites practitioner experience, disagreement, or examples.>

#<tag1> #<tag2> #<tag3>
```

Checklist:

- Hook is first-hand, not generic.
- The post teaches one useful idea even if nobody clicks.
- The canonical link uses the documented UTM convention.
- The question is specific enough for practitioners to answer.
- Hashtags are few and relevant.
- Privacy review is complete.
