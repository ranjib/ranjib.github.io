# LinkedIn Carousel Template

Use this for a native 6-8 slide PDF carousel. Keep text large, plain, and legible on mobile.

## Source Outline

```markdown
# <Essay title or thesis>

## Slide 1: Title/thesis
<One-sentence thesis.>

## Slide 2: Shared failure mode
<Name the problem.>

## Slide 3: Contrast or mapping
<Show the useful comparison.>

## Slide 4: First practical layer
<One idea, one example.>

## Slide 5: Second practical layer
<One idea, one example.>

## Slide 6: Operational loop
<How the idea improves over time.>

## Slide 7: Warning and invitation
<One caution. Point to the canonical article.>
```

## Requirements

- 6-8 slides.
- One idea per slide.
- Mobile-readable text.
- Accessible contrast.
- Final slide points to the canonical article with a campaign URL using `utm_content=carousel`.
- No private context or audience-specific notes.
