# Measurement Plan

## Principle

Measure whether the site reaches the intended SRE, platform, AI-engineering, maker, and cross-domain audiences without adding invasive tracking. Outcomes matter more than raw page views.

## Review Windows

For major essays, review at:

- 48 hours: launch resonance and obvious metadata/search issues.
- 7 days: conversation quality, referrals, saves, and indexed URL state.
- 30 days: durable search impressions, returning readers, citations, invitations, and open-source collaboration.

## Metrics

LinkedIn:

- out-of-network reach;
- profile views and followers gained from the post;
- saves and sends;
- substantive comments;
- visits to external links;
- audience role or seniority when available.

Website/search:

- Search Console impressions and clicks;
- indexed pages and structured-data errors;
- campaign visits from generated UTM links;
- returning readers when available from an approved privacy-first provider;
- RSS/newsletter subscription trend;
- high-intent outcomes such as speaking invitations, citations, or open-source collaboration.

- Keep the scorecard owner-maintained; do not automate collection from private dashboards into this public repository.

- If a privacy-first analytics provider is later approved, capture only aggregate data needed for the scorecard.
